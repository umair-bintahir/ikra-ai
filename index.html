THE IKRA INQUIRY ENGINE

Learning doesn't move in a straight line.

IKRA guides each learner through a continuous cycle of questioning, exploration, reasoning and reflection — while AI adapts the journey along the way.

01
QUESTION

Start with curiosity.

IKRA begins with something worth wondering about, not an answer to memorize.

02
EXPLORE

Investigate possibilities.

Learners explore ideas, evidence and different ways of looking at the problem.

03
REASON

Challenge the thinking.

IKRA uses questions and prompts to surface assumptions and encourage deeper reasoning.

04
REFLECT

Make sense of what changed.

Learners reflect on what they discovered, how their thinking evolved and what they now understand.
