The Problem

Today's classrooms rely on passive learning and memorization. Students search for answers instead of developing critical thinking skills. Traditional tools provide information but don't teach children how to think, question, and learn independently.

Passive Learning

Students consume information without engaging in deeper inquiry.

Limited Personalization

One-size-fits-all approach doesn't adapt to individual learning styles.

No Real-Time Insights

Teachers lack data-driven understanding of student progress and learning gaps.

How IKRA Works

IKRA stands for Inquiry, Knowledge, Reflect, Analytics

I

Inquiry

AI guides students to ask meaningful questions and explore topics deeply through structured inquiry-based prompts.

K

Knowledge

Students build understanding by accessing curated resources and AI-powered explanations tailored to their learning level.

R

Reflect

Guided reflection prompts help students consolidate learning and think critically about what they've discovered.

A

Analytics

Real-time learning analytics provide teachers and parents with insights into progress, engagement, and growth areas.

The Learner Journey

How students experience inquiry-based learning with IKRA-AI

1

Start with a Question

Students begin with an open-ended question or learning objective, encouraged to think independently.

2

Explore & Discover

AI provides personalized learning paths with resources, simulations, and guided challenges.

3

Think & Analyze

Students work through problems, experiment with ideas, and develop deeper understanding.

4

Reflect & Consolidate

Guided reflection helps students articulate learning and connect concepts.

5

Grow & Progress

Analytics track growth, and the AI adapts the next learning journey based on progress.
